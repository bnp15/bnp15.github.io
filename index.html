About BNP15

The Bayesian nonparametrics (BNP) conference is a bi-annual international meeting bringing together leading experts and talented young researchers working on applications and theory of nonparametric Bayesian statistics. It is an official section meeting of the Bayesian nonparametrics section of the International Society for Bayesian Analysis (ISBA). Attendees are expected to share our commitment to safeISBA, and to adhere to the ISBA code of conduct.

BNP15 will be held from the 28th June to the 2nd July 2027 at the Sant'Agostino campus of the Università degli studi di Bergamo in the city of Bergamo (Italy).

All attendees are expected to adhere to the ISBA code of conduct.

Information about the previous edition can be found here.

Venue

Sant'Agostino Campus
Piazzale Sant'Agostino 2, 24129, Bergamo, Italy

The Sant'Agostino Campus is home to the Department of Human and Social Sciences and the Aula Magna of the University of Bergamo. It is a former monastery and one of the few historic buildings that survived the urban redevelopment that continued until the 16th century.

The Sant'Agostino Campus is located in Città Alta (Bergamo's historic upper town). Below are some of the ways to reach the campus.

From Bergamo airport (Orio al Serio)
- Take BUS Line 1 from "Aeroporto Il Caravaggio" to "Vittorio Emanuele" (4 min walk).
- Take a Taxi to "Ex monastero Sant'Agostino".
From Bergamo train station
- Take BUS Line 1 from "Papa Giovanni" to "Vittorio Emanuele" (4 min walk).
- Walk 35 min
From Bergamo Porta Nuova
- Take BUS Line 1 from "Porta Nuova Chiesa Grazie" to "Vittorio Emanuele" (4 min walk).
- Walk 25 min
